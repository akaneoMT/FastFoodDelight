## FastFood Delight 2.0.0 — Minecraft 26.3 (Fabric)

### Port
- Ported from Forge 1.19 to **Fabric 26.1**
- Now requires **Farmer's Delight Refabricated** and **Fabric API**

### New
- **Beet Fries**: cooked in the Cooking Pot with a beetroot and a Fries Bowl, also sold by the Fast Food Waiter
- **Checkout Machine recipe**: it can now be crafted (iron ingots, glass, redstone and paper)
- **Recipe book**: recipes now unlock in the recipe book and the Cooking Pot book as you collect their ingredients
- **Config file** (`config/fastfooddelight-common.json`): enable or disable Fast Food shops in villages, and set how often they appear
- Fast Food Waiters keep their uniform when turned into zombie villagers
- Items are added to Farmer's Delight and common tags (snacks, meals, sweets, drinks, foods) for better compatibility with other mods
- The Fries Bowl can be shift-clicked into the Cooking Pot's container slot

### Changes
- **Cheese** is now made in the Cooking Pot: 4 cheese per milk bucket, 1 hunger point each
- **Apple Sauce** now needs a bowl and 2 sugar
- **Checkout Machine** behaves like the Cooking Pot: lantern sounds, breaks quickly, drops even without a tool
- Fries, ice creams and apple sauce now stack to 16, like Farmer's Delight meals
- Recipes use common tags: any bread, leafy green, onion, tomato… from other mods works
- Fast Food shops are a bit rarer in villages (weight 15 instead of 25, configurable)
- Village shops now spawn villagers the vanilla way: an unemployed villager shows up and becomes a Fast Food Waiter
- Waiter trades are now data-driven and can be changed with a datapack

### Fixes
- The Checkout Machine can now be mined with a pickaxe and drops itself
- Crafting flavored ice creams no longer duplicates bowls
- Apple Sauce no longer gives a free bowl
- Removed the stray leatherworker villager from every Fast Food shop
- French translation fixes ("Oignons frits", "Croque-monsieur")

This update was made with the help of AI (Claude, by Anthropic), under the direction of the original author, who guided every step and reviewed the changes.

Thanks to Rinko1231 for the unofficial 1.20/1.21 update, which inspired some of these changes.
